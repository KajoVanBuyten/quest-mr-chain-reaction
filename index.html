Chain Reaction

Build Rube-Goldberg machines across your real furniture. Dominoes, marble ramps, bowling pins, bumpers, seesaws and explosive barrels — your room scan becomes the physics world. Place the glowing trigger ball, hit start, and watch it all go down in bullet time.

Features

  • Your room is the level — detected planes & room mesh become Rapier colliders.
  • Hit-test placement with a glowing ghost preview snapped to real surfaces.
  • Domino line tool — drag along the table to lay a curved row.
  • Grab & throw anything with hands or controllers.
  • Bullet time, replay of the last run, combo counter & perfect-run confetti.
  • Explosive barrels with shockwave, glow flash and sparks.
  • Procedural spatial audio — spatial clicks, rolling marbles, booms. No sound files.
  • Four preset levels auto-placed on your largest table or the floor.
